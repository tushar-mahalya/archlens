ARCHLENS - SEE THE SYSTEM

Original 24-second launch-film design and composition for Archlens.
Product: https://github.com/tushar-mahalya/archlens
Source content: Archlens v0.1.1 examples and current product distribution
information; MIT, copyright Tushar Sharma.
The depicted order-service library is fictional. Its runtime behavior and
proposed ADR are actual repository examples. The styled document views are
not recordings of a live agent session or a separate Archlens application.
The visual identity and lens mark were created for this film.
